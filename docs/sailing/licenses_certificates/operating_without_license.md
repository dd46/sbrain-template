# Operating Boats Without a License (Poland)

**Patent ≠ dowód osobisty.** A patent (certificate) proves you passed an exam. Your **ID card or passport** is what you show at a rental desk to sign the contract — it does not grant navigation rights by itself.

Without any patent you can still legally operate certain boats under Polish law (rozporządzenie MSiT on water tourism). You remain fully responsible for knowing navigation rules, safety equipment, and right-of-way.

## Motorboats — two legal paths without SM patent

### Path A: small engine (most common)

| Parameter | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| Engine power | **up to 10 kW** (~13.6 HP) |
| Hull length | **no statutory limit** (practically small fishing boats, dinghies, RIBs) |
| Waters | inland + sea (within general rules) |

Examples: small outboard fishing boats, electric motors, tender boats.

### Path B: slow “houseboat” (Mazury, canals)

All three conditions must be met **simultaneously**:

| Parameter | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| Hull length | **up to 13 m** |
| Engine power | **up to 75 kW** (~100 HP) |
| Max speed | **15 km/h**, limited by **construction** (not just throttle) |
| Waters | **inland only** |

When renting this type, the operator must give you a **safety briefing** (typically 30–60 min) before handover. Rental companies often also require you to be **18+**.

## Sailboats without patent

| Parameter | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| Hull length | **up to 7.5 m** |
| Auxiliary engine | **any power** — engine does not change the limit; hull length counts |
| Waters | inland; coastal rules still apply for where you may sail |

Above 7.5 m you need [[sailing/licenses_certificates/polish_sailing_license|żeglarz jachtowy]] (inland) or JSM (sea).

## What always needs a patent

| Boat type | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| Motorboat **>10 kW** (and not qualifying as houseboat path B) | Requires [[sailing/licenses_certificates/polish_motorboat_license|sternik motorowodny]] |
| Sailboat **>7.5 m** | Requires sailing patent |
| **Jet skis / personal watercraft (skuter wodny)** | Patent required — no exemption |
| Towing water-skier / inflatables | SM patent + separate towing license |

## What you need “on the ID alone” at rental

Even without a patent, bring:

1. **Dowód osobisty or passport** — identity for contract and police checks
2. **Signed rental agreement** and handover protocol
3. **Safety briefing** — mandatory for houseboats; often required by rental policy for all boats
4. **Age** — many rentals require 18+ even when law allows 14+ with patent

The rental company may refuse you even if the boat qualifies for no-patent use (their policy).

## On-board documents and equipment

Patent-free does not mean rule-free:

- **Registration / CE documents** for the boat (if applicable)
- **Insurance** (OC) — usually arranged by rental
- **Life jackets** for everyone on board
- **Sound signal** (whistle/horn), fire extinguisher, paddles/oars — depending on boat size
- Longer boats need more gear (first aid kit, flares, etc.)

## Quick decision table

| I want to… | Without patent? |
|------------|-----------------|
| Small motorboat, engine ≤10 kW | Yes |
| Houseboat on Mazury, ≤13 m, ≤75 kW, ≤15 km/h | Yes (+ briefing) |
| Sailboat ≤7.5 m (with or without engine) | Yes |
| Motorboat with 40 HP outboard | No — need SM |
| 10 m sailboat | No — need żeglarz jachtowy |
| Jet ski | No — need SM |
