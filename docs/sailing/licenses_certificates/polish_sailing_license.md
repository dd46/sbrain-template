# Polish Sailing License (Żeglarz Jachtowy)

The **patent żeglarza jachtowego** is the entry-level recreational sailing certificate in Poland. It is issued by **PZŻ** (Polski Związek Żeglarski), not PZMWiNW (which handles motorboat licenses).

Boats up to **7.5 m** can be operated without any license. Above that, or on coastal/marine waters, you need a certificate.

## Requirements

- Minimum age: **14 years**
- No mandatory training course (but strongly recommended)
- No medical examination required
- No prior sailing experience or logged hours required

## What it allows

- Inland waters (lakes, rivers — Mazury, Zegrze, etc.)
- Coastal waters up to **2 nautical miles** from shore
- No engine power limit when sailing; auxiliary engine use is covered

## Exam

Two parts on the same day, administered by a PZŻ examination board:

1. **Theory** — multiple-choice test (regulations, navigation, meteorology, safety)
2. **Practical** — on-water maneuvers (departure, docking, sail handling, MOB)

Schools typically bundle a 3–7 day course with the exam ("pod klucz").

## After passing

1. Generate application at [patenty.pya.org.pl](https://patenty.pya.org.pl)
2. Pay online, print the form
3. Mail: application + exam certificate + photo (3.5 × 4.5 cm)
4. Patent arrives by post in ~2–4 weeks

## Costs (approximate, 2026)

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Course + exam (bundle) | 1 800 – 3 300 PLN |
| Patent issuance | see PZŻ fee schedule |

## Higher levels (for later)

| Level | Min. age | Key requirement |
|-------|----------|-----------------|
| Jachtowy sternik morski (JSM) | 18 | 200 h of sea sailing |
| Kapitan jachtowy | 18 | JSM + extensive sea log |

## When you also need a motorboat license

A sailing certificate covers auxiliary engine use on a sailboat. For a **dedicated motorboat**, see [[sailing/licenses_certificates/polish_motorboat_license]].
