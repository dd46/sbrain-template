# Polish Motorboat License (Sternik Motorowodny)

The **patent sternika motorowodnego (SM)** is the entry-level license for operating motorboats in Poland. It is issued by **PZMWiNW** (Polski Związek Motorowodny i Narciarstwa Wodnego) — a separate body from PZŻ (sailing).

## Requirements

- Minimum age: **14 years**
- No mandatory training course (but recommended)
- No medical examination required
- No prior experience required

## What it allows

- **Inland waters** — all motorboats
- **Sea** — motorboats up to **12 m** hull length, **daytime only**, within **2 NM** of shore
- **Under 16 years old** — engine power limited to **60 kW**

Without SM you can still operate smaller boats — see [[sailing/licenses_certificates/operating_without_license]] (≤10 kW motor, houseboat path, sail ≤7.5 m).

## Exam

Full syllabus: [[sailing/licenses_certificates/sm_exam_syllabus]]. Summary below.

Two parts on the same day:

### Theory

- 75 multiple-choice questions, 90 minutes
- Minimum **65 correct** answers to pass
- Topics: inland navigation rules, boat construction, engines, maneuvering, pilot books (locje), meteorology, water rescue, environmental protection, navigation aids, first aid

### Practical

- Docking and departure maneuvers
- Anchoring
- MOB (man overboard)
- Engine operation and basic troubleshooting
- Knots and line handling

## After passing

1. Apply online at [motorowodniacy.org](https://www.motorowodniacy.org/patenty.html)
2. Attach photo (3.5 × 4.5 cm), pay issuance fee
3. Patent is **permanent** (no renewal)

## Higher levels (for later)

| Level | Min. age | Key requirement |
|-------|----------|-----------------|
| Motorowodny sternik morski (MSM) | 18 | SM + 200 h sea sailing (min. 2 voyages) |
| Kapitan motorowodny | 18 | MSM + 1 200 h sea log + additional voyages |

## Additional licenses

| License | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| Water-ski / towable toy towing | Separate license; min. 18 + SM patent |
| Motorboat mechanic | Separate exam (25 questions, 30 min) |

## Sailing vs motorboat — quick comparison

| | Sailing (PZŻ) | Motorboat (PZMWiNW) |
|---|---|---|
| Entry certificate | Żeglarz jachtowy | Sternik motorowodny |
| Min. age | 14 | 14 |
| Course required | No | No |
| Medical exam | No | No |
| Sea (entry level) | ≤2 NM from shore | ≤2 NM, boats ≤12 m |

For sailboats with auxiliary engine, a sailing certificate is sufficient. For dedicated motorboats, you need SM.
