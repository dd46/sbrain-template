# SM Exam Syllabus (What to Learn)

Official source: **Załącznik nr 4, część III** to rozporządzenia MSiT o uprawianiu turystyki wodnej. See [[sailing/licenses_certificates/polish_motorboat_license]] for age, fees, and what the patent allows after passing.

## Exam format

| Part | Format | Pass threshold |
|------|--------|----------------|
| **Theory** | 75 questions, single choice (A/B/C), 90 min | **≥65 correct** (max 10 errors) |
| **Practical** | Selected maneuvers on motorboat, ~20–30 min | All assigned elements correct (one retry per failed element) |

Both parts on the **same day**. You must pass both.

Question pool: PZMWiNW maintains **700+ questions** across categories — exam draws 75. Courses and apps drill the full pool.

## Theory — 10 official topics

### 1. Przepisy prawa drogi (~30–40% of exam)

The largest block. Highest error rate in practice.

- Right-of-way on inland and coastal waters
- Meeting, overtaking, crossing situations
- Speed limits, restricted zones, MPZZM rules
- Registration, documents, operator obligations
- Sound and light signals

### 2. Budowa jachtu motorowego

- Hull types, deck layout, terminology (szpigat, reling, etc.)
- On-board equipment and safety gear
- Steering gear, fuel system basics

### 3. Silniki i układ napędowy

- Engine types: **zaburtowy vs stacjonarny**
- Start/stop procedure, routine maintenance
- Propellers (pędniki) — basics
- Common faults and safe responses at sea

### 4. Manewrowanie

- Effects of wind and current on a motorboat
- Turning, stopping distance, pivot point
- Safe speed in harbour vs open water
- Jet ski rules (theory — SM covers PWC regulations)

### 5. Locja (pilotage / waterway knowledge)

- **Navigation marks** — cardinal (N/E/S/W), lateral, special
- Reading maps and pilot guides (locje, przewodniki)
- Inland waterways, locks, weirs, bridges
- Hydrotechnical structures on Polish rivers and lakes

### 6. Ratownictwo wodne

- Safety equipment on board — use and legal requirements
- Capsize, collision, engine failure procedures
- **MOB (człowiek za burtą)** — theory and approach side
- First aid basics, hypothermia recognition and response
- WOPR, SAR services — who to call

### 7. Meteorologia

- **Beaufort scale** — wind force numbers
- Weather signs (clouds, fronts, sudden wind shifts)
- Weather bulletins for navigation

### 8. Pomoce nawigacyjne

- Compass, GPS/chartplotter basics
- Position, course, speed
- Reading a map at pilotage level (not full MSM navigation task)

### 9. Ochrona wód

- Waste disposal rules, no-discharge zones
- Fuel/oil spill prevention
- Protected areas on Polish waters

### 10. Sygnalizacja (often grouped with przepisy)

- Sound signals (horn, bell)
- Light signals at night
- Distress signals

## Practical — what the commission tests

The examiner picks elements from the official list. Typical session:

### Before leaving the dock

- Pre-departure check: life jackets, fire extinguisher, buoy, fuel, engine run
- Brief the “crew” — commands and roles

### Maneuvers (subset assigned to you)

| Maneuver | What they watch |
|----------|-----------------|
| **Odejście od nabrzeża** | Safe push-off, clear prop, controlled departure |
| **Dojście do nabrzeża** | Approach speed, wind/current compensation |
| **Kurs prosty + cyrkulacja** | Steady course, controlled 360° turn |
| **Spotkanie / wyprzedzanie** | Correct side, safe passing distance |
| **Człowiek za burtą** | Stop, return on **leeward** side, recover dummy/buoy |
| **Kotwiczenie** | Choose ground, scope, set and check hold |
| **Cumowanie** | Throw/pass line, secure bow/stern, fenders |

### Crew and seamanship

- Give clear commands at the right moment
- Work as crew member when instructed
- **Basic bosun work** — knots (cleat hitch, bowline-level), handling lines

One **retry** allowed per failed practical element.

## Study plan (practical order)

1. **Przepisy + znaki** — pass/fail topic; drill until ≥90% on mock tests
2. **Locja** — cardinal marks and map symbols; flashcard heavy
3. **Silnik** — start/stop, fuel, zaburtowy vs stacjonarny
4. **Ratownictwo + MOB** — procedures by heart
5. **Meteorologia** — Beaufort numbers
6. **Reszta** — budowa, ochrona wód, pomoce nawigacyjne
7. **Woda** — 2–3 days maneuvers if doing a course; otherwise rent practice hours before exam

## Prep resources

- PZMWiNW question database (via training schools / apps)
- Optional course: 3–5 days theory + water (not legally required)
- Mock exams: aim for **≥70/75** consistently before real exam

## After passing

Patent application at [motorowodniacy.org](https://www.motorowodniacy.org/patenty.html). See [[sailing/licenses_certificates/polish_motorboat_license]].
